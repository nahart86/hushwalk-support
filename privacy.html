Your walk belongs to you

Privacy, with room to breathe.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Hushwalk is designed to work entirely on your iPhone. It does not require an account, connect to Hushwalk servers, or use analytics, advertising, or tracking. Hushwalk does not collect or transmit your saved walk content. Answers for a stop are written to the local archive only after both people submit them.

What stays on your iPhone

Answers from completed stops, favorites, and saved walks are stored locally on the iPhone where you use Hushwalk. Hushwalk does not receive a copy. Anyone who can unlock and use your iPhone may be able to see content saved in the app.

Answers for the current stop are held only in the active app session until both people submit. If Hushwalk closes before submission, those two answers are lost and need to be entered again. Earlier completed stops remain saved on the device.

Sharing is your choice

You can choose to export a keepsake using the iOS share sheet. Hushwalk only passes it to a destination after you choose to share. The app or service you select handles the content under its own privacy practices.

Permissions

The Hushwalk experience does not ask for access to your camera, location, or microphone. The core activity does not need an internet connection.

Retention and deletion

Hushwalk does not keep a server-side copy of your walk content. Submitted answers, favorites, and keepsakes remain in the app's local data on your iPhone. Removing Hushwalk and its local app data removes the copy Hushwalk stores on that device. Apple device backups and any destination you choose when exporting are governed by the settings and terms for those services; Hushwalk cannot retrieve or delete copies stored there.

Changes to this policy

If Hushwalk's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the new practices.
