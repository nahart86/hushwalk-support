Pick three or five stops. At each one, take turns writing a short answer before you see each other’s.

One iPhone, passed between you

Write first.
Reveal together.

Each person gets a quiet moment to answer. When you’re both ready, open the reveal and read what the other noticed.

Hushwalk keeps drafts and completed walks on your iPhone. There are no accounts to set up and no connection needed for the walk.
