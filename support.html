We're here to help

Let’s make the next walk smoother.

Hushwalk is an offline activity for two people sharing one iPhone. Choose three or five stops, write short responses privately, then reveal them together.

Quick help

  • Did Hushwalk close while you were answering? Reopen the same walk on the same iPhone. Earlier completed stops remain saved; if both people had not submitted the current stop, enter both answers again.
  • Looking for a saved response? Completed stop answers and keepsakes stay on the iPhone where you created them. Hushwalk does not sync them between phones.
  • Want to save a keepsake elsewhere? Choose the export action in Hushwalk, then select a destination in the iOS share sheet.
  • Need a connection? The core walk is designed to work offline. You do not need an account to use it.
Contact support

Open a support issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include private walk responses, passwords, or personal details. Describe the issue and include your iPhone model and iOS version if useful.
